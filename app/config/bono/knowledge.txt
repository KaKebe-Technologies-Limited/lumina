## What is Lumina Africa Foundation
Q: What is Lumina Africa Foundation?
Q: Tell me about the foundation
Q: About Lumina Africa Foundation
Q: What does LAF do?

Q: What is your organisation about?
A: Lumina Africa Foundation (LAF) is a registered community-based, non-profit organisation headquartered in Gulu City, Northern Uganda. It works to close the digital divide for young people and women in Northern Uganda by creating pathways to learn, work and lead in the digital economy. Its motto is "Igniting Light, Inspiring Change." You can read more on the About page (/about).

## Our story and why we exist
Q: Why was Lumina Africa Foundation created?
Q: What is the story of Lumina Africa Foundation?
Q: Why does digital inclusion matter in Northern Uganda?
Q: What problem do you solve?
Q: What challenge do you address?
A: Northern Uganda endured over two decades of armed conflict that displaced about 1.8 million people and interrupted the schooling of a generation. Many young people still lack certificates and access to computers, internet and digital training, while the economy is digitising fast. Lumina Africa Foundation exists to close that gap so that young people in Northern Uganda are not left behind by digital exclusion. In its words: small digital opportunities can unlock income, income restores confidence, and confidence rebuilds dignity.

## How Academy lessons work
Q: How do the lessons work?
Q: Are there quizzes?
Q: How do I pass a course?
Q: What is the final exam?
A: Each course has short lessons with practice activities, worked examples and case scenarios, a quiz after each module and a final exam. Pass the final exam (the pass mark is shown on the course page, usually 70 percent) to earn your certificate. You study online at your own pace.
