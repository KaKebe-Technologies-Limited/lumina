Lumina Africa Foundation is a registered community-based, non-profit organisation headquartered in Gulu City, Northern Uganda, Reg. No: GSD/R 1234. 
We were established by a team of young African changemakers committed to addressing one of the region’s most urgent challenges, youth unemployment driven by digital exclusion in a post-conflict environment. 
Northern Uganda endured over two decades of armed conflict that displaced approximately 1.8 million people and disrupted education systems for an entire generation. Many young people today continue to face the long-term consequences of interrupted schooling, limited certification, and restricted access to technology. 
As the world rapidly evolves through digital transformation, the gap between those with access to digital skills and those without continues to widen. 
Lumina Africa Foundation exists to close that gap.

“Northern Uganda lost years to conflict, but we will not lose our future to digital exclusion. Every young person, regardless of certificates or background, deserves the skills to participate in today’s economy. When we equip youth with practical digital tools, we restore dignity, rebuild confidence, and unlock opportunity.” 
Moses Komakech, Founder

💡 Who We Are 
Vision 
An inclusive digital future for Northern Uganda where all young people  and women are equipped, connected, and empowered to thrive in the global digital economy. 
Mission 
“To reduce youth unemployment in Northern Uganda by equipping vulnerable young people with market‑relevant digital and employable skills, providing psychosocial support, and creating pathways to decent work and sustainable livelihoods.” 
Core Values 
✅ Equity & Inclusion 
✅ Integrity & Transparency 
✅ Community-Driven Action
