Frequently Asked Questions 
How can I donate? 
You can donate via mobile money, bank transfer, or international donations through PayPal/Stripe. 
Is my donation tax-deductible? 
Please check with your local tax authority regarding the deductibility of donations made to our organization. 
What programs do donations support? 
Donations support school supplies, digital literacy training, and community health outreach programs for children and families. 
Can I donate monthly? 
Yes, you can set up a recurring donation to support our ongoing programs. 
Who can I contact? 
You can contact us through our website for any inquiries or assistance regarding donations. 
What is the impact of my donation? 
Every contribution directly helps us provide essential resources and programs to children, youth, and families in need. 
Support Us 
Join us in making a significant difference for families in our community and beyond. Together, we can build a supportive environment that encourages growth, resilience, and hope for every family. Your involvement is essential in helping us achieve our goals and create lasting change. Let's work hand in hand to transform the lives of those who need it most.
