Careers at Lumina Africa Foundation 
At Lumina Africa Foundation, we believe in people. Our impact is driven by passionate individuals who dedicate their time, talent, and energy to transforming lives. Whether you are a volunteer, intern, or professional, joining our team means becoming part of a movement that is lighting the path to opportunity for youth, women, and marginalized communities in Northern Uganda and beyond. 
Why Join Us? 
Be part of a purpose-driven organization focused on health, education, digital innovation, leadership, and sustainable development . 
Gain valuable experience working directly with communities and partners. 
Receive mentorship and growth opportunities while contributing to real impact. 
Join a team that values integrity, collaboration, and innovation .

Our Recruitment Process 
Submit your application via our online form. 
Applications are reviewed by the HR & Programs team. 
Shortlisted candidates are contacted for interviews or assessments. 
Successful candidates are onboarded and supported through training and orientation. 
Equal Opportunity Statement 
Lumina Africa Foundation is an equal opportunity organization. We celebrate diversity and are committed to creating an inclusive environment for all staff, volunteers, and partners. 
Get Involved Today 
Your skills, passion, and dedication can change lives. Join us and be part of the journey to empower communities and inspire the next generation 
✨ At Lumina Africa Foundation, this is more than a career. It’s a calling to serve, inspire, and transform. 
Career Page FAQs 
How to apply for jobs? 
Visit our career page and submit your application online. 
What updates will I receive? 
You will receive notifications about new job openings and HR activities. 
Can I update my application? 
Yes, you can log in to your account and update your application details anytime. 
What is the application process? 
The process includes submitting your resume and completing an online form. 
Who can I contact for help? 
You can reach our HR team via the contact form on our website.
